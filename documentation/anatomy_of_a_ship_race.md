**Anatomy of a ship race**

I've realised that I go through a number of phases when learning any new ship race.

1. Learning the waypoints
> “This makes no sense” . “Why am I flying in circles looking for this blasted gate”. “Grrr, I hate this”

2. Familiarisation
> “Ah that makes sense now”, “okay, this is doable”

3. Setting down some good finish times
> “Oh, I can fit in a boost here”. “I love this course!”

4. The crash and burn
> Boost. Splat. Ship rebuy. *"You need to fly back to the starting body"*. Repeated over and over.

5. Testing the boundaries
> *“Your altitude was too high for this checkpoint”*. Over and over. Lots of restarts. Not many times are set in this phase.

6. Repetition and refinement
> Finally the zen phase is reached where the above still occur, but less frequently. The times that are set are minor increments, but these ones matter the most.

Do you recognise any or all of these phases?
