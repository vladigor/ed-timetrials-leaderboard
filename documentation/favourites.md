# Favourites & Ignored Races

The favourites feature lets you mark races as **❤️ Favourite** or **💔 Ignored** so you can personalise the race list to your own preferences.

## How to mark a race

Open any race page and click the heart button near the race title. Each click cycles through three states:

| Icon | State | Meaning |
|------|-------|---------|
| 🤍 | Unmarked | Default — no preference set |
| ❤️ | Favourite | A race you care about |
| 💔 | Ignored | A race you want to hide |

Your preferences are saved in your browser and persist across sessions.

Note: If you use more than one browser or device, your preferences will not show on the other device.

## What it changes on the race list

Once you have races marked, two things become available on the home page:

- **Sort: Favourites first** — sorts ❤️ races to the top, 💔 races to the bottom, with everything else sorted by recent activity in between.
- **Hide 💔 races** checkbox — filters out any race you've marked as ignored. This checkbox only appears once you have at least one ignored race.

## What it changes on the cmdr page

The Improvement opportunities section on the cmdr page now shows favourited races first.

## Things to know

- Preferences are stored locally in your browser. They are **not** tied to your commander profile and won't sync across devices or browsers.
- This is because we don't have log ins for the site; we just adopt the cmdr name on first access. This method prevents someone else from assuming your identity and changing your favourites.
- Clearing your browser's site data will reset all preferences.
- The feature is currently in early access and not available to all commanders.
